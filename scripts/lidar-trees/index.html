LiDAR trees

Finds every tree in the USGS 3DEP point cloud (Kalamazoo 2015 flight, public domain, served as Entwine Point Tiles from usgs-lidar-public) over the zoom-14 tile range below. The points that are neither ground nor on an OpenStreetMap building footprint form a canopy height model on a 2 m grid; each local maximum of that model that looks like a crown rather than a pole or a wire becomes a tree with a height, a crown radius and a fullness (the share of crown cells that returned points, which in a leaf-off flight separates dense conifers from bare deciduous crowns: it is the share of pulses over the crown that the crown stopped before the ground).

Everything runs in this page (Overpass and the S3 bucket both send CORS headers, laz-perf decodes LAZ in WebAssembly). It streams roughly 1.5 GB and then downloads kalamazoo-trees.json.gz, which scripts/build-tree-tiles.mjs turns into the tiles under public/trees.
